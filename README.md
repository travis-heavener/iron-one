### Travis Heavener

## About
I wanted to experiment with pure vibecoding by s    ying out of the codebase as much as possible here.
I'm using Antigravity CLI to develop this SPA.

I've been lifting since October 2023, and in recent months it's been more off and on.
A big part of it is the lack of accountability and the headache of keeping my splits apart.

IronOne (hosted on my homelab named "mini-one") is the solution to this problem.
Leveraging Tailscale, this product syncs workout progress on the homelab so that progress is available between devices.
